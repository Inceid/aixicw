# Beyond Chat

## The present and future landscape of clinical AI

Artificial intelligence in medicine is not new. What is changing is the emergence of large, reusable **foundation models**, **generative AI**, and increasingly **agentic systems** that operate across text, images, audio, electronic health records, and other biomedical data.

This companion site offers a practical framework for understanding that landscape: where clinical AI came from, what systems are used today, what emerging systems may make possible, and how medical students can evaluate their use.

> [!abstract] The central question
> What specific benefits, risks, and tradeoffs arise from **this system**, for **this population**, in **this setting**?

## The throughline

Clinical AI systems differ in their data, purpose, autonomy, evidence base, and risk. “AI” is therefore less useful as a single category than as a starting point for asking what a particular system actually does.

Use the [[scorecard|scorecard]] to identify the **problem, data, output, predictors, performance, and consequences**. Then ask whether the evidence and safeguards fit the intended clinical use.
