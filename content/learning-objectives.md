---
title: Learning Objectives
description: Objectives across ethics, equity, implementation, and research.
---

## Ethics and professionalism

- **Identify** ethical and professional responsibilities related to AI use, including transparency, accountability, privacy, and preservation of clinician judgment.
- **Evaluate** when AI use may alter informed consent, responsibility, or the clinician–patient relationship.

## Equity and population health

- **Identify** how bias can enter AI systems through data, measurement, model design, access, and deployment.
- **Evaluate** whether an AI system’s benefits and harms are distributed equitably across patient populations.

## Clinical implementation

- **Identify** implementation safeguards, including human review, escalation pathways, monitoring, and post-deployment evaluation.
- **Evaluate** whether an AI tool is appropriate for a specific clinical workflow based on evidence, safety, usability, and oversight requirements.

## Research and data analysis

- **Identify** common analytic limitations in AI research, including confounding, data leakage, poor target selection, dataset shift, and inadequate validation.
- **Evaluate** clinical AI studies for validity, generalizability, subgroup performance, and clinically meaningful outcomes.
