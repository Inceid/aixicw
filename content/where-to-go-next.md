## Ethics and professionalism

- [Algorithmic Justice League](https://www.ajl.org/) — research, public education, and advocacy addressing algorithmic discrimination, accountability, and societal harms.
- [American Medical Association Medical Student Section](https://www.ama-assn.org/member-groups-sections/medical-student-section-mss) — student policymaking and advocacy on emerging issues, including AI.
- [Coalition for Health AI](https://www.chai.org/) — frameworks for transparency, safety, accountability, assurance, and organizational governance.
- [MIT AI Risk Repository](https://airisk.mit.edu/) — a structured source for identifying and classifying AI risks.

## Equity and population health

- [NIH AIM-AHEAD Consortium](https://www.aim-ahead.net/) — training, mentorship, and research opportunities focused on AI/ML, health equity, and broader participation in biomedical data science.
- [Data for Black Lives](https://d4bl.org/) — researchers, organizers, and community members using data science to confront racial inequity and strengthen community power.
- [MIT Critical Data](https://criticaldata.mit.edu/) — collaborative datathons and educational programs applying data science to health problems, with an emphasis on global access.

## Clinical implementation

- [MD+](https://www.mdplus.community/) — a student community focused on physician innovation, hackathons, and clinical-biomedical implementation.
- [American Medical Informatics Association](https://amia.org/communities/working-groups/student) — trainee communities and career pathways in informatics and implementation.
- [Digital Medicine Society](https://dimesociety.org/) — practical toolkits and communities focused on evaluating, implementing, and scaling digital health technologies.

## Research and data analysis

- [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/) — open clinical datasets, tutorials, software, and annual challenges for learning signal processing and machine learning.
- [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/) — shareable models, datasets, tutorials, and demonstrations. Check licensing and documentation before reuse.
- [Stanford Center for Artificial Intelligence in Medicine and Imaging](https://aimi.stanford.edu/) — open clinical datasets, seminars, and examples of responsible development.
- [Sage Bionetworks Challenges](https://challenges.synapse.org/) — open challenges for developing, benchmarking, and validating translational biomedical AI tools.

## Technical foundations

- [A Few Useful Things to Know About Machine Learning](./resources/A%20Few%20Useful%20Things%20to%20Know%20About%20Machine%20Learning.pdf) — an accessible conceptual introduction included with this site.
- [Stanford AI in Medical Education resources](https://med.stanford.edu/ai-in-meded/resources-and-tools.html)
- [AAMC principles for AI use in medical education](https://www.aamc.org/about-us/mission-areas/medical-education/principles-ai-use)

> [!warning] Use public tools carefully
> “Openly available” and “open source” do not mean clinically validated, secure, or privacy-preserving. Do not upload protected health information, patient-level data, confidential educational materials, or restricted research data to public tools. Verify outputs and follow applicable institutional policies.
